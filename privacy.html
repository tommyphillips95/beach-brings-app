Beach Brings — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026 · Beach Brings LLC, South Texas Gulf Coast · tommyphillips95@gmail.com

Beach Brings LLC ("Beach Brings," "we," "us") operates the Beach Brings app and website. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Account info — name, phone number, and email when you sign up.
  • Delivery location — the GPS pin or address you choose for a delivery. Location is used only to fulfill and route your order.
  • Order details — items, amounts, and delivery notes.
  • Payment — card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers; Stripe handles payment data under its own policy.
  • Driver applicants — vehicle details, license, insurance, and background-check status, used to onboard and verify drivers.
  • Device & usage data — basic app/analytics data to keep the service working and improve it.

How we use it

  • To take, route, and deliver your orders, and connect you with a driver.
  • To process payments and pay drivers and partners.
  • To send order updates by SMS or email (via Twilio and SendGrid).
  • To provide support and prevent fraud or misuse.

Sharing

We share only what's needed to provide the service: your first name and delivery pin with the assigned driver; order details with the fulfilling partner; and data with our processors (Stripe for payments, Twilio/SendGrid for notifications, Supabase for hosting). We may disclose information if required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Location

Location is collected only when you set a delivery spot or, for drivers, while on duty to route deliveries. You can disable location in your device settings, though delivery features won't work without a drop location.

Your choices & rights

You can update or delete your account, opt out of marketing messages, and request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing tommyphillips95@gmail.com. Texas residents and others have rights under applicable privacy laws.

Children

Beach Brings is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
